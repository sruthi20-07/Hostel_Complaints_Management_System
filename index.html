Prototype Architecture & Limitations

Fix My Hostel is an offline-capable client-side prototype. It relies on standard browser localStorage to persist complaint tickets, public updates, warden notes, and notices across reloads.

  • Storage is scoped to your current browser profile on this machine. Data will not sync across other physical devices.
  • The demo role switcher simulates both student and warden workflows using the same shared complaint database.
  • Internal warden notes are strictly isolated and never rendered in student views or exported to public CSV files.
  • Complaint IDs (e.g. FMH-2026-0001) are generated incrementally based on highest existing recorded numbers.

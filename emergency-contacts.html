    Emergency Call

    When to Call vs When to Submit an Online Ticket

    Call Immediately For:

    • Smoke, burning smell, or active electrical sparks.
    • Major water pipe burst flooding rooms or corridors.
    • Medical emergency or student injury.
    • Security breaches, broken exterior doors, or unauthorized entry.

    Use Web Complaint System For:

    • Slow-dripping water taps or clogged washroom basins.
    • Wi-Fi router offline or weak connectivity.
    • Loose desk screws, chair repairs, or cupboard hinges.
    • Corridor bulb replacement or geyser heating element servicing.
